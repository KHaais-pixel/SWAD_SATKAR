# Swad Satkar — design direction

**Concept: overhead, one cool lamp.** The whole site is a tabletop seen from
directly above, lit by a single cool light off to the upper-left. Every surface
is the dark blue table (`--ink`), something raised on it (`--char`), or china and
paper catching that light. Shadows fall down-right, highlights land upper-left,
everywhere. That single light source ties both signature animations to one room.

**Palette, taken from the restaurant's own card.** The card is a saturated azure
with white foil, so azure became the accent that everything you can act on is
made of: buttons, the rating, the leader lines, the drawn page rule. The grounds
are the same hue pushed down to near-black, and the menu stock is a cool printed
white rather than cream. The one rule that gives the palette its tension: **the
room is blue and the food is the only warm thing on the table.** Chili red, basil
green, egg yolk and brass serviceware keep their own colour and read as heat in a
cold room.

**Type.** Fraunces at high optical size for anything a diner would read off a
card — dish names, headings — set tight so it feels printed, not web-y. Inter for
wayfinding and UI. Noto Serif Devanagari carries स्वाद सत्कार beneath the Latin
wordmark at 60%.

**Motion.** The menu book turns under the lamp: the specular sweep across the leaf
is light catching curved paper, and its shadow falls onto the page beneath. The
plate turns under the same lamp: the rim highlight stays fixed while the dish
rotates, so the room is still and only the food moves, lagging the type like a
heavy dish being set down. Both say the same thing: you are seated, the table is
set, things are being brought to you.
